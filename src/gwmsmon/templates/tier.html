How to read this

Config Share is the static fraction the admin set in HTCondor. Shares at a tier sum to 100%.

Cores Used / % of Pool is what the group is actually running right now (sum of users' WeightedResourcesUsed).

Expected is the share pure byquota would give this group: ConfigQuota / sum(ConfigQuota of demanding groups). A group is "demanding" if its Requested exceeds its EffectiveQuota — i.e. it actually wants more than its base share. Non-demanding groups release their quota as surplus.

Fulfillment is actual / expected:

  • ≈ 1.0: getting roughly what byquota predicts.
  • < 0.85 (red): underserved. Could be matchmaking failure (jobs' requirements don't fit available slots), time-throttle, or a negotiator-config constraint not visible here. The signal flags the symptom — diagnosing the cause needs deeper inspection of the negotiator (NegotiatorLog, slot inventory).
  • > 1: absorbing some underserved group's missed share. Not bad in itself; the pool is conserved so what one group is missing, others are picking up.

Requested is total weighted demand (idle + running), straight from the accounting ad.

(unclaimed slots) is a real count from condor_status: startds at this tier in State == "Unclaimed". These are slots the negotiator could match right now but no job has claimed them. A large unclaimed row together with a low Fulfillment for some group means real matchmaking headroom exists.

(other: timing skew, ungrouped, …) is whatever's left of Pool − Σ Cores Used − unclaimed. Comes from the EffectiveQuota and WeightedResourcesUsed snapshots being from slightly different moments, slots in transit between matches, or users with no accounting group.

Caveat: pure byquota is the model the per-group SurplusPolicy=byquota setting claims. The negotiator's actual algorithm also depends on GROUP_AUTOREGROUP, GROUP_SORT_EXPR, per-cycle time caps, and so on — Fulfillment shows the deviation but doesn't explain its cause.
