How to read this

Config Share is the static fraction set in HTCondor. Quotas at a tier sum to 100%. With SurplusPolicy=byquota and GROUP_AUTOREGROUP=True, a group can exceed its static share by absorbing siblings' idle quota.

Cores Used / % of Pool is what the group is actually running right now (sum of its users' WeightedResourcesUsed).

Requested is the group's total weighted demand — idle plus running. If Requested ≫ Cores Used the group has unmet demand and is being throttled by the negotiator. If Requested ≈ Cores Used the group is demand-limited (it could grab more but has nothing to schedule).

Priority / Eff. Prio is the group's fair-share Priority. It's an exponentially-decayed EMA of recent usage, multiplied by PF (PriorityFactor). LOWER is better — the negotiator serves low-priority groups first. A group that's been hammering the pool recently has a high Priority; it decays back over time (PRIORITY_HALFLIFE).

Lifetime Usage is cumulative since the group's BeginUsageTime (often years ago). It does NOT drive surplus decisions — only the decayed Priority does. A group can have low lifetime usage but a high current Priority (and vice-versa).

Between-group surplus distribution depends on the negotiator's config (GROUP_SORT_EXPR, GROUP_AUTOREGROUP, per-group SurplusPolicy) which isn't visible from the accounting ads. Pure quota-proportional and pure priority-proportional are bracketing extremes; the actual share-out is somewhere between and depends on those settings.
